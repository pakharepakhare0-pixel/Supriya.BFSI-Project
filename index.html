5 Key Factors That Shape Credit Health

Credit bureaus synthesize these institutional dimensions when computing your credit rating:

1. Payment History 35% Impact

The single largest factor. Track record of on-time loan EMIs and credit card bill payments.

2. Credit Utilization Ratio 30% Impact

The portion of available revolving credit you spend. Best practice is keeping utilization below 30%.

3. Length of Credit History 15% Impact

Average age of all open credit accounts. Older accounts demonstrate seasoned borrowing responsibility.

4. Credit Mix (Secured vs Unsecured) 10% Impact

Healthy balance of secured credit (home, car loans) and unsecured credit (credit cards, personal loans).

5. New Credit Inquiries 10% Impact

Hard inquiries made by lenders when you apply for credit. Avoid applying for multiple cards or loans simultaneously.

ℹ️ Educational Note: Scoring algorithms differ slightly between credit bureaus (such as CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark, and FICO). This gauge illustrates standard benchmark tiers.
